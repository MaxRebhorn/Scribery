## Page 1 — A Few Words Before the Words {#preface}

An alphabetical lexicon is wonderful if you already know the word. But if you already know the word, you don't need a lexicon.

That's the problem.

This book solves it. Instead of the alphabet, the words are sorted by what they're about. Light and darkness. Food. Family. Walking about. You know, the usual.

Every one of the 1005 words from the Gospel of John appears here exactly once. Not twice. I'm not made of ink.

If you can't find a word in the theme where you expect it, try another. Greek words have a habit of wandering.

And if you still can't find it, check the Little Words. Everything small ends up there.

Each entry works as in the other lexica: the word, its sounds in Latin letters, what kind of word it is, and what it means.

Within a theme, the words are sorted by their Latin spelling, so you needn't know the Greek alphabet to find anything. Although it helps. It always helps.

Happy browsing.

— John Koine
