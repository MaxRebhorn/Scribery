## Page 1 — A Few Words Before the Words {#preface}

So you've learned the letters. And you've survived the grammar. Frankly, I'm impressed.

Now you'd like to know what the words actually mean. Naturally.

This lexicon covers alpha, beta and gamma. That's three letters out of twenty-four.

I know. But a proper education doesn't come cheap, and I have to split these things up somehow. It keeps the price tolerable. For you. Not for me.

All 185 words in this book appear somewhere in the Gospel of John. I haven't padded it with words you'll never meet. Mostly because then I'd have to look them up.

Each entry gives you the word as a dictionary lists it, its sounds in Latin letters, what kind of word it is, and what it means.

Where a Greek word has several meanings, you get the one that matters most here. The rest you'll have to find out for yourself. Builds character.

You'll notice that many Greek words begin with a tiny mark above the first letter. Those are breathings. They are not contagious.

Right. Off you go.

— John Koine
